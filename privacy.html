Pix Bot - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pix Bot may process, how it is used, and how it is handled.

2. Information We May Process

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Server ID
  • Discord username or display name
  • Pix currency balance
  • Experience points and level information
  • Information required to operate bot commands

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide and maintain bot features such as profiles, virtual currency, transfers, levels, leaderboards, and server functionality.

4. Data Storage

Data may be stored to preserve user and server settings between bot restarts. We do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary for operating the bot.

5. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell or rent user information. Information may be processed or disclosed when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security of the service.

6. Discord

Pix Bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's platform and developer policies.

7. Data Deletion

Users or server administrators may contact the developer to request deletion of data associated with Pix Bot where applicable.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data handling practices change.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the developer through the official support channel associated with Pix Bot.
